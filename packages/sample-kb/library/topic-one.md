## Stance

- Maintainer burnout is a real, observable pattern; it is a symptom
  of missing funding, not of missing process.
- "Just fork it" is not a substitute for paying the maintainer a
  stipend.
- GitHub Sponsors + corporate line-item funding is the most under-used
  combination.

## Recurring references

- Nadia Eghbal, *Working in Public* (2020) — the default text on this.
- xkcd #2347 — the dependency-tower illustration.

## Sample reply shapes

- *Counter-point to "OSS is free":* "Free-as-in-beer, not free-as-in-
  cost-of-maintenance. The maintainer's time is the hidden line item."
- *Pro-sponsorship cue:* "Most people don't realise a $10 / month
  sponsorship covers a meaningful chunk of the project's infra bill."

## Phrases to avoid

- "Just open a PR" — dismissive; ignores the reviewer cost.
- "Maintainers owe the community nothing" — technically true but
  starts fights.
