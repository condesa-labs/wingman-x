## What I know

- Subscription (capital call) facilities: borrowing base on uncalled
  commitments, investor eligibility, exclusion events.
- NAV facilities, hybrids, GP financing, and how they interact with fund
  documents and LP consent.
- Where fund finance sits in a manager's liquidity stack and why lenders
  care about reporting more than about the assets.

## What I believe (DRAFT — confirm or edit)

- Fund finance is a reporting business. The lender's real risk is stale
  or unverifiable data about commitments and NAV; better data pipes lower
  the cost of borrowing more than better collateral does.
- Tokenized fund interests make the most sense when they simplify the
  lender's job: verifiable ownership, transfer restrictions in code, and
  NAV that can be attested on a schedule.

## Where I push back (DRAFT)

- "Tokenized LP interests unlock a secondary market." The bid comes from
  a buyer who can diligence the fund; tokens do not change that.
