# Tokenized equities and onchain capital markets

<!-- Chime In seed. DRAFT positions below are for you to confirm, edit, or delete. -->

## What I know

- The models for tokenized equities: native onchain issuance with the
  chain as register, wrapped or synthetic exposure, and depositary-style
  receipts.
- Corporate actions, dividends, voting, and shareholder communications
  as the workload a tokenized equity register has to carry.
- Where onchain issuance genuinely lowers cost (private companies with
  messy cap tables, cross-border distribution) versus where it does not
  (large-cap public equities).

## What I believe (DRAFT — confirm or edit)

- Tokenized public equities mostly solve a distribution and access
  problem for specific venues; the plumbing case is much stronger for
  private company shares where the cap table is the problem.
- An equity token that is not the register is a synthetic, and should
  carry synthetic-instrument disclosures.
- Onchain capital markets will be built by issuers who want programmable
  compliance and distributions, not by traders who want another venue.

## Where I push back (DRAFT)

- Headlines about "tokenized stocks" that are wrapped exposure with no
  shareholder rights.
