## What I know

- How institutions actually adopt digital assets: through a mandate
  change, an approved custodian, a counterparty list, and an operations
  runbook — in that order, and slowly.
- The gap between what a protocol can do and what an investment committee,
  compliance team, and auditor will sign off on.
- Institutional requirements: segregated custody, bankruptcy remoteness,
  audited controls, reporting that reconciles to the general ledger.

## What I believe (DRAFT — confirm or edit)

- Institutional adoption is gated by operational and legal
  infrastructure, not by interest. The demand has existed for years; the
  approved path did not.
- The first institutional use cases are boring on purpose: collateral
  mobility, intraday settlement, fund administration. Trading venues get
  the attention; back-office savings get the budget.
- "Institutional grade" means an auditor can trace every balance to a
  control. Anything else is marketing.
- Permissioned rails and public chains will coexist; the question is
  which layer holds the record of record.

## Where I push back (DRAFT)

- "Institutions are coming" as a thesis without naming the workflow they
  will run.
- Announcements of pilots presented as adoption.
