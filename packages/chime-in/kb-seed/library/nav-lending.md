## What I know

  eligibility and concentration tests, valuation frequency and disputes,
  cash sweep and cure mechanics.
- The difference between lending against a diversified fund portfolio and
  against a concentrated position, and how lenders price it.
- Why valuation cadence and independence are the entire credit story.

## What I believe (DRAFT — confirm or edit)

- NAV lending against tokenized positions is one of the clearest onchain
  financing use cases: the collateral is a position whose ownership,
  transfer restrictions, and valuation attestation can all live on the
  same ledger the lender reads.
- The value-add is faster, more frequent NAV attestation with a clear
  audit trail — not a new legal structure.
- Margin and eligibility logic that runs against attested NAV reduces
  disputes; disputes, not defaults, are what make NAV facilities slow.

## Where I push back (DRAFT)

- NAV lending pitched as "DeFi for funds" without a valuation agent,
  dispute mechanism, or LP consent path.
