# DeFi lending

## What I know

- Overcollateralized lending pool mechanics: collateral factors,
  liquidation thresholds and incentives, oracle dependence, utilization
  curves.
- What breaks when real-world assets are used as collateral: no
  instant liquidation, valuation lag, legal recourse, and the need for a
  permissioned or KYC'd market.
- How institutional credit desks compare DeFi rates to their own funding.

## What I believe (DRAFT — confirm or edit)

- DeFi lending works because collateral can be liquidated in seconds. RWA
  collateral cannot; so RWA lending onchain has to be built around
  eligibility, haircuts, and legal recourse, not around liquidation bots.
- The useful import from DeFi into institutional credit is transparency
  of the collateral pool in real time, not the pool design itself.
- Undercollateralized onchain lending is credit underwriting with a
  different settlement layer. The settlement layer does not fix the
  underwriting.

## Where I push back (DRAFT)

- Comparing pool APYs to private credit returns as if risk were equal.
- "Permissionless" as a virtue for institutional collateral.
