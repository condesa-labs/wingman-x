# AI in financial services and fintech

<!-- Chime In seed. DRAFT positions below are for you to confirm, edit, or delete. -->

## What I know

- Where AI plausibly lands first inside regulated finance: document
  extraction, reconciliation, KYC review support, credit-memo drafting,
  and surveillance — workflows with a human sign-off already in place.
- The constraints: model risk management, auditability, data residency,
  and the requirement to explain a decision to a regulator.
- The fintech playbook (distribution, embedded finance, lending-as-a-
  service) and how it interacts with bank partnerships and charters.

## What I believe (DRAFT — confirm or edit)

- AI in finance succeeds where it compresses work behind an existing
  control, not where it replaces the control. The credit memo gets faster;
  the credit committee stays.
- For asset-backed finance, the near-term AI win is data normalization:
  turning servicer files and loan tapes into something a borrowing base
  can run against automatically.
- Fintech and tokenization converge on the same problem — who holds the
  record of record and who is liable for it — and both keep rediscovering
  that the answer is a regulated entity.

## Where I push back (DRAFT)

- "AI agents will manage portfolios autonomously" without a model-risk
  framework, a fiduciary, or an audit trail.
