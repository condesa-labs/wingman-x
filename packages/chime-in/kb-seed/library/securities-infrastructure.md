## What I believe (DRAFT — confirm or edit)

- The win from onchain settlement is intraday collateral mobility and
  the removal of reconciliation, not "instant settlement" as a headline.
  Many participants want the option of T+0, not the obligation.
- Settlement is only as atomic as its slowest leg. Without an onchain cash
  leg accepted by both sides, DvP onchain is a promise to reconcile later.
- Market infrastructure changes when a regulated infrastructure provider
  runs the ledger, not when a startup mirrors it.

## Where I push back (DRAFT)

- Pilot announcements reported as production settlement.
- "T+0 for everything" — funding and liquidity management need the
  settlement window.
