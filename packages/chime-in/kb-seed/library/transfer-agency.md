## What I know

- What a transfer agent actually does: maintains the register of record,
  processes transfers and restrictions, handles distributions and
  corporate actions, and reports to issuers and regulators.
- How an onchain register can serve as the official books and records,
  and what has to be true legally and operationally for that to hold.
- Investor onboarding, whitelisting, and the reconciliation burden when
  the chain and the register disagree.

## What I believe (DRAFT — confirm or edit)

- The transfer agent is the quiet center of tokenized securities. If the
  transfer agent does not treat the chain as the register, the token is a
  receipt, and every "onchain" claim downstream inherits that weakness.
- The real product of a tokenization platform is a register that
  regulators, auditors, and lenders will accept as authoritative.
- Transfer restrictions enforced in code are a genuine improvement over
  restrictions enforced by email and legal opinion — this is where
  tokenization removes work rather than relocating it.

## Where I push back (DRAFT)

- Platforms that describe themselves as "tokenizing" while the register
  lives in a database and the token is synced nightly.
